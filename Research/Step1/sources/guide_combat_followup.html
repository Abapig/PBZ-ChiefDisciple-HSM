Combat System Guide

Parry, dodge, weapon combos, Phantom Edges, executions and the four difficulty modes, assembled from official footage and hands-on reports, with the same confidence labels the trackers use.

Clips are short muted loops from S-GAME’s official upload of the State of Play deep dive (Aug 17, 2026). Each links to the moment it comes from. Footage © S-GAME.
挡

Parry

Demo Verified

Parry is the spine of the system, not an optional defensive tool. Successful parries interrupt enemy chains and open the damage windows that combos are built around. Chinese gameplay analysis shows the window tightening measurably on the higher difficulty tier rather than enemies simply gaining health.

Source: IGN 中国 实机演示全解析 (Bilibili, Jul 2026); State of Play deep dive, Aug 17, 2026
闪

Dodge

Demo Verified

Dodge covers what parry cannot: unblockable attacks flagged with a red tell. Lateral dodges consistently outperform backwards ones against area attacks, since shockwave radii in demo footage exceed the visible animation.

Source: Gamescom demo footage, Aug 2025; State of Play deep dive, Aug 17, 2026
连

Weapon Combos & Matchups

Demo Verified

Soul carries 30 primary weapons and 25 Phantom Edges, and each weapon has its own chains rather than a shared moveset. The White Serpent & Crimson Viper twin blades end a combo by flinging the shorter blade as a projectile; the Soft Snake Sword deflects a hit just before it lands and heavily cuts the attacker’s Sha-Chi, and can form a blade barrier that severs thread-type attacks. Weapons are also matchups: S-GAME says a light blade is not enough against heavy shields, which call for the momentum of iron fists and hammers. Upgrading a weapon unlocks new forms and techniques, and a Reforging Talisman resets a weapon’s level and refunds the materials.

Phantom Edges

Demo Verified

Phantom Edges are the secondary weapons, 25 in total, used alongside the primary weapon. They cover what blades cannot. Four are confirmed in Phantom Edge slots on the in-game equipment screen: the Night Owl longbow, the Man-Cutter axe, the Flaming Mane flamethrower, which clears packs of dogs, and the Bashpole sledgehammer dropped by Wan Jun “Coppermaul”. The Tiger Cannon, a hand cannon that blasts apart distant enemy formations, sits in the R2 Phantom Edge slot in the State of Play footage and was listed as a Phantom Edge in TGS 2024 hands-on coverage.

Execution Finishers

Demo Verified

Low-health enemies and bosses become eligible for a scripted finisher. In the demo build these were cinematic and unskippable, and functioned as a reward for the parry loop rather than a separate resource system.

Source: Gamescom demo footage, Aug 2025; State of Play deep dive, Aug 17, 2026
难

Difficulty Modes

Confirmed

There are four modes. Wayfarer is “a gentler path through the Wulin”: focus on the story and fight at your own pace. Pathbreaker is the normal mode. Hellwalker brings in fighting-game mechanics: bosses drop fixed patterns and react to your spacing, punish whiffed attacks and adapt to your habits. 66 Days (Sixty-Six Days) plays at Hellwalker difficulty with only 66 lives: every defeat costs a day, and when the clock reaches zero the death is permanent. It unlocks after completing the game on Pathbreaker or Hellwalker.
