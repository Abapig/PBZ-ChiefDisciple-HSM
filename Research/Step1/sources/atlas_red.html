How to beat Red, The Mysterious Swordswoman

Cramped arena.

The arena is cramped, but guides call the fight easy. Dodge her red-flash backflip strike and her dagger throw; for the throw, wait a moment until she lands, because it comes out slightly after the backflip. Block the Air Step Slash combo and land a perfect parry on its last hit to open her up for a counter. Watch for her jump to the spire, and when crimson threads bind you, get your block or parry ready, since a gap-closing red attack always follows. One reviewer found that Ghoststep dodges break her guard quickly.

Phases

No health-phase changes are listed in the guides we checked.

How to get there

Dovecote bell is the only landmark given; Game8 does not describe the climb to the tower.

Attacks to watch for

  • One-Two Slash to Backflip: two quick slashes then an unblockable retreating strike; dodge it.
  • Dagger Throw: she backflips and launches a fast unblockable projectile; wait for the red glint and dodge only after she lands.
  • Air Step Slash: flurry of slashes while seemingly walking on air; block it, and a perfect parry on the final hit opens her up.
  • Crimson Thread: after binding you with threads she always follows with a gap-closing strike; prepare block or parry in advance.
  • Leap to the spire: she jumps up to the central spire of the roof. Watch her closely, because a thread grab and a red attack can follow.

Weak points

Ghoststep dodges break her guard quickly (one source). A perfect parry on the last hit of Air Step Slash opens her for a counter.

Before the fight

  • Rest at the nearest bell first. It refills your health and items and becomes your respawn point. See how saving works.
  • Spend your upgrade materials before the fight. A reforge refunds them later, so you lose nothing by trying a different weapon. See how to upgrade weapons.
  • Stuck? Drop the difficulty for one attempt and raise it again afterwards. You can change it at any time.

Rewards

No item reward listed; progresses the Daughter of the Dark Sect side quest (Game8)
