Difficulty Modes

There are four modes: Wayfarer, Pathbreaker, Hellwalker and 66 Days. The first three are available from the start; 66 Days unlocks after you finish the game on Pathbreaker or Hellwalker. The descriptions below are the game's own menu text, read off S-GAME's State of Play.

Last verified Nov 5, 2026 · From official footage and launch-week reports; retail checks are added with their dates.

Our suggestion, based on how S-GAME describes each mode.

Wayfarer

Story mode
“A gentler path through the Wulin. Focus on the story, take in the world, fight at your own pace.”In-game menu text

The mode for players who are here for the story and the world. S-GAME's own pitch: "If you'd simply like to enjoy the story, Wayfarer mode is for you."

ConfirmedSource: State of Play deep dive 11:06; PlayStation Blog, Aug 17, 2026

Pathbreaker

Normal
On-screen description not shown yet.

The normal mode, second on the menu. On normal difficulty, bosses keep fixed combos you can learn: Liang says a boss like Huangxing "has some of the Soulslike elements to him" there. Finishing the game on Pathbreaker unlocks 66 Days.

ReportedSource: State of Play deep dive 11:06 (menu order), 11:48 (unlock rule); Gamereactor, Aug 2026 ("the normal mode"); Liang via GamesRadar+, Sep 21, 2025

Hellwalker

Hard
“The ultimate trial. Enemies strike without mercy, adapt without warning, and hold nothing back.”In-game menu text

The difference is behaviour, not just health bars. S-GAME borrowed from fighting games for boss AI: on Hellwalker a boss reads whether the situation favours him, and decides his next move from whether his last attack landed. Fixed patterns stop being reliable. Finishing on Hellwalker also unlocks 66 Days.

ConfirmedSource: State of Play deep dive 11:16; PlayStation Blog, Aug 17, 2026; Liang via GamesRadar+, Sep 21, 2025

66 Days

Post-game challenge
“Hellwalker difficulty, with one added rule: you have only 66 lives. Unlocks after completing PATHBREAKER or HELLWALKER.”In-game menu text

Also called Sixty-Six Days mode. It ties the story's 66-day countdown to your deaths: every defeat takes a day off the clock, and when it reaches zero the run is over for good. You cannot start the game on it; it opens once you have finished Pathbreaker or Hellwalker.

ConfirmedSource: State of Play deep dive 11:48 and 12:10; PlayStation Blog, Aug 17, 2026

Pathbreaker vs Hellwalker

Start on Pathbreaker unless you already parry by habit. Hellwalker changes how bosses fight more than how hard they hit, and it punishes guarding. What launch-week guides report, with their sources:

Boss behaviour
Hellwalker bosses stop repeating fixed combos and react to the situation and to whether their last attack landed. On Pathbreaker they keep combos you can learn.
Source: Liang via GamesRadar+, Sep 21, 2025
Confirmed
Boss movesets
Some boss attacks are replaced by harder versions on Hellwalker, not just added on top, per AllThingsHow, which names The Hunt, Peng Meng, Bai Chilian, Chang Hong and Wan Jun.
Source: AllThingsHow, updated Nov 1, 2026
Reported
Guard and Sha-Chi
Blocking costs more on Hellwalker: AllThingsHow puts it at roughly 1.5 times the Sha-Chi per blocked hit. Push Square: "proper use of parries is necessary to break guards in a timely manner."
Source: AllThingsHow, updated Nov 1, 2026; Push Square, Oct 28, 2026
Reported
Enemy health
Ordinary enemies have 25% more health on Hellwalker, per AllThingsHow; it lists elite and boss health as unchanged.
Source: AllThingsHow, updated Nov 1, 2026
Reported
Damage you take
The two guides disagree. AllThingsHow: a hit "deals no more damage than on Pathbreaker", and the perfect-deflect window stays the same. Push Square: enemies "deal more damage" on Hellwalker. We have not measured it.
Source: AllThingsHow, updated Nov 1, 2026; Push Square, Oct 28, 2026
Reported
What stays the same
Both unlock 66 Days when you finish the game on them, and neither is needed for any trophy: the PS5 list has no difficulty trophy.
Source: State of Play difficulty menu 11:48; PSNProfiles trophy list, Oct 29, 2026
Confirmed
Neither guide says it measured these in the retail game, and they disagree on damage taken; we have not tested it ourselves.

New Game+ (New Cycle)

Finishing the game opens a new playthrough, called a New Cycle. Only what a source states is listed; how much harder each cycle gets is not confirmed, so it is left out.

What it is called
New Cycle. The results screen at the end of a run (we checked the one for the 66 Days ending, Undying Grudge) offers "New Cycle" or "Return to Title Screen" and shows a Cycle counter.
Source: BabyZone, All 4 Endings, 27:52 (retail results screen)
Confirmed
How to start it
After the true ending, speak to Inanis to start New Game+.
Source: PC Gamer, Sean Martin, Oct 30, 2026
Reported
Difficulty
You can pick a different difficulty when you start it.
Source: PC Gamer, Sean Martin, Oct 30, 2026
Reported
What carries over
Your weapons and Phantom Edges. Nothing else is confirmed yet; we will add it once a source shows it.
Source: PC Gamer, Sean Martin, Oct 30, 2026
Reported
Before you start it
You can also keep playing in free roam after the final boss and clear what is left; failed Tales stay failed.
Source: PowerPyx trophy roadmap (Oct 29, 2026); DTG Reviews (Oct 30, 2026)
Reported
Before starting a new cycle, check the missable quests: quests you failed do not come back. The four endings, including 66 Days' Undying Grudge, are on the endings page.

FAQ

Should I play Pathbreaker or Hellwalker?

Start on Pathbreaker unless you already parry by habit in action games. It is the normal mode, it unlocks 66 Days just like Hellwalker, and no trophy needs Hellwalker. Hellwalker changes how bosses fight, not just their numbers: they adapt instead of repeating combos, and launch-week guides report that blocking costs more Sha-Chi, so it rewards deflecting over guarding. It also makes a good second run.

How many difficulty modes does Phantom Blade Zero have?

Four: Wayfarer, Pathbreaker, Hellwalker and 66 Days. All four appear on the difficulty menu shown in the State of Play deep dive. S-GAME's PlayStation Blog post only named three of them, which is why some sites still say three.

How do you unlock 66 Days mode?

Finish the game on Pathbreaker or Hellwalker. The menu text reads: "Unlocks after completing PATHBREAKER or HELLWALKER." Wayfarer is not listed as unlocking it.

What happens when you run out of days in 66 Days mode?

Each death costs one of your 66 lives, shown as days. When the count reaches zero the run ends on its own ending, Undying Grudge; the results screen gives the reason as failing "to uncover the truth and find the “Heart” before the end of your sixty-six-day lifespan". See the endings page (/guides/endings/#undying-grudge).

Does Phantom Blade Zero have New Game Plus?

Yes, called New Cycle. PC Gamer: after the true ending, speak to Inanis to start New Game+; you can change the difficulty, and your weapons and Phantom Edges carry over. What else carries over has not been confirmed.

What does Hellwalker change?

Enemy behaviour. Bosses stop following fixed combos and use fighting-game style AI that reacts to the situation and to whether their last attack hit. S-GAME describes it as more advanced, adaptive enemy intelligence.

Can you change the difficulty mid-game?

Yes, outside combat, according to AllThingsHow (November 2026): open SYSTEM, go to the Game tab and choose Select Difficulty; during a fight the menu says the action cannot be performed. Two cautions. At S-GAME's October 2026 media event, Inven Global found that after dropping to the easiest difficulty it could not switch back up, so keep a separate save before lowering it. And 66 Days cannot be switched to mid-game: Push Square says it has to be chosen when starting a new game.

Is Wayfarer an easy mode?

It is the gentlest option, described on screen as "a gentler path through the Wulin" where you fight at your own pace. S-GAME pitches it at players who want to enjoy the story.
